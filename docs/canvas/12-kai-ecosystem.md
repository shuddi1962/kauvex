## DIFFERENTIATOR: KAI AS A BUSINESS PARTNER

Instead of acting only as a conversational assistant, KAI should function as an operational partner.

It should proactively identify risks, surface opportunities, automate repetitive work, coordinate specialized AI agents, and help organizations make better decisions while keeping humans in control of important approvals.

---

## AI CODING INSTRUCTIONS

Before implementation:

1. Build KAI as a distributed ecosystem of specialized AI services coordinated by a Master Orchestrator.
2. Keep each AI capability modular and installable.
3. Integrate KAI deeply with the Marketplace, Business OS, K3D Engine, and every Design Studio.
4. Support multimodal inputs, retrieval from company knowledge, and extensible AI providers.
5. Ensure privacy through strict tenant isolation and configurable permissions.
6. Preserve existing Kauvex functionality while making KAI the intelligence layer across the entire platform.
