# K TRUST, SECURITY & COMPLIANCE PLATFORM (K TRUST)

> Canvas Document 17 - The security, privacy, governance, identity, compliance, and resilience platform that protects every user, organization, AI agent, Design Studio, Digital Twin, and business operating on Kauvex. Builds on Canvas Documents 1-16.

## OBJECTIVE

K Trust is the security, privacy, governance, identity, compliance, and resilience platform that protects every user, organization, AI agent, Design Studio, Digital Twin, and business operating on Kauvex.

Security should not feel like an add-on. It should be built into every feature from day one.

---

# CORE PHILOSOPHY

Every user should trust the platform.

Every organization should own its data.

Every action should be auditable.

Every AI action should be explainable.

Every system should be secure by default.

---

# ZERO TRUST ARCHITECTURE

Implement Zero Trust principles throughout the platform.

Never automatically trust:

* Users
* Devices
* Networks
* APIs
* AI Agents
* Third-party integrations

Continuously verify identity and permissions.
